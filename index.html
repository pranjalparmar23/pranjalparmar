Automated Detection of Steel Manufacturing Defects Using Machine Learning Techniques

Pranjal Parmar and Team

Companion research paper to the Steel Manufacturing Defects project. Proposes a machine learning pipeline — CNN for defect classification, ResUNet for image segmentation, and ResNet50 for defect localization — trained on a dataset of 12,997 steel images. The system moves quality control from traditional manual inspection (under 70% accuracy) to proactive, automated defect detection, reducing rework, waste, and production downtime.
