Overview

Project Overview

The Internship Portal implements personalized user accounts allowing individuals to easily update and track their internship status, upload certificates, and get a fostered user-friendly experience throughout. Teachers can update internship availability as well, making it a two-sided platform for both students and faculty.

Capabilities

Key Features

Personalized Student Accounts

Individuals track and update their own internship status from a dedicated account.

Certificate Uploads

Students can upload completion certificates directly to their profile.

Teacher Management View

Teachers can update and manage internship availability for students.

Responsive UI

Styled with Tailwind CSS for a consistent experience across devices.
